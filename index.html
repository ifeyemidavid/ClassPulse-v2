How It Works

Live in under 60 seconds

No app. No account for students. Just a room code and a browser. The simplest path from zero to full classroom intelligence.

1
Lecturer

Create a session & upload your notes

Log in, create a classroom session, and optionally upload your PDF lecture notes. ClassPulse AI reads the content instantly.

2
AI

AI generates questions from your material

Gemini AI extracts key concepts from your notes and creates multiple-choice questions with plausible distractors. You review before launching.

3
Student

Students join with a 6-digit room code

Students open any browser, enter the room code and their name. No sign-up, no app install. They're live in 5 seconds.

4
Lecturer

Watch the classroom come alive in real time

See every student's answer as it happens. The confusion meter, live bar charts, and AI analytics report tell you exactly who needs help — and what to re-teach tomorrow.

Features

Everything a teacher needs.
Nothing they don't.

Confusion Meter

Students anonymously signal "Got it", "Unsure", or "Lost" in real time. Teachers get a live heatmap — not an exam result three weeks later.

AI Question Generator

Upload a PDF or paste lecture notes. Gemini AI generates quiz questions tuned to your exact content. You always review before publishing.

Live Analytics Dashboard

Real-time bar charts, response rates, and per-student status. See which answer options students are picking as they pick them.

AI Session Report

When the session ends, an AI report identifies weak topics, students needing intervention, and which questions to re-teach. Done in seconds.

Works on 2G Networks

2-second polling instead of WebSockets. Sub-2s load time. Under 100KB initial payload. Designed for real African infrastructure — not Silicon Valley fibre.

AI Study Buddy

Students chat directly with AI about the exact topic taught in class. Not generic ChatGPT — a tutor scoped to today's lesson.
